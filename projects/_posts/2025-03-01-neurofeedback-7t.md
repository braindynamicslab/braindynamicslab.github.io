### Aims

<!-- TODO: expand this section with project details -->
This project conducts a feasibility study of advanced neurofeedback techniques using high-resolution 7T MRI in patients with treatment-resistant depression (TRD). Manish Saggar serves as Co-Principal Investigator, with MPIs Drs. Cammie Rolle and David Spiegel. The study includes a randomized controlled trial enrolling more than 200 patients.
